Wordly Games News

Wordly Games was created in 2025 with the goal of creating an anagram game that was reminiscent of the ones we remember from the early days of Android phones. In the process of researching modern games we noticed that most word games in the Google Play Store were full of ads or had in-app purchases. We made the decision early that we would not bog our game down with any of that and decided to create a premium experience that was owned free and clear by its users. In February of 2026 we officially released Andygrams Anagram Word Puzzles, and it is now enjoyed by players in 32 U.S. states and 8 countries!

We began to learn that selling a premium game - even at just $2.99 - was a pretty steep barrier to entry for a lot of people. It is much more difficult to market a paid app than it is a free one, so we made a minor pivot and introduced Andygrams Daily Challenge. This allowed players to try the Andygrams experience for free in a fun, daily puzzle version.

But it also meant we needed to have ads and a way to remove them so that players had an option for which type of play they wanted - paid or free. So our original goal of being a company creating ad-free games went right out the window! We did limit Andygrams Daily Challenge to one ad that can be skipped, and an additional optional ad that isn't necessary for gameplay at all. This turned out to be a great solution, and we understand why modern game developers make the decisions that they do.

Enter Ricochet.

Another one of our plans was to concentrate strictly on word games. (Hence the name - Wordly Games) But that plan also changed one day when going through an old phone that had the game Orbital by Bitforge Studios on it.

Orbital was a fantastic game that was inspired by the free Flash game Gimme Friction Baby by Wouter Visser. This type of game has sadly been missing from the Google Play Store for some time now, so we decided to bring the genre back with our own fresh take — plus a few new twists! Ricochet will be launching soon, and will be completely free to play in Normal mode with minimal skippable ads. Three additional modes - Gravity, Geometry, and Escalation - along with game options such as Zen mode will be able to be unlocked for just $2.99. Google Play Games is also integrated, letting you earn achievements and Play Games XP!

We never imagined when we started that we would have three games in the Google Play Store. Feedback for Andygrams has been overwhelmingly positive, and early tester feedback for Ricochet has been even better! We're so excited for you to try any of our games, and can't wait to hear what you think!
