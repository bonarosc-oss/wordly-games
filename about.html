Our Story

Wordly Games is an independent Android development studio based in Clermont, Florida. Founded with a love of classic word games, the goal is simple: create clean, engaging, and highly polished word puzzles that bring back the nostalgic feel of old games you used to play that no longer work on your modern Android devices.

The Technology

Every title, including Andygrams Anagram Word Puzzles and Andygrams Daily Challenge, is built natively from the ground up. By utilizing modern Android frameworks, the games deliver smooth animations, responsive interfaces, and a lightweight footprint on your devices. Our games support phones, tablets, and foldables.

Player-First Philosophy

We created Andygrams Anagram Word Puzzles to be your go-to anagram game anytime, anywhere. By operating completely offline there are no ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no limitations to your gameplay. With options to play with or without a timer, three difficulty levels, and expert mode, there are many ways to customize your play. You can even add your own words to the dictionary and remove words that you don't want in future games.

Andygrams Daily Challenge was born as a way to allow people to try out the Andygrams experience for free. It is a daily, ad-supported version that provides a single new timed puzzle every day, and all players are trying to find the same words. Games last between 3 and 5 minutes, and you can share your score card with friends and family to compete for daily and weekly high scores! Ads can be removed, and there is a 50% discount if you already own Andygrams Anagram Word Puzzles!

Looking Ahead

Our goal is to keep the games as simple as we can. If and when we add features they are optional and off by default. That way you can keep experiencing the games as you like. We'll make sure that we're updated to modern Android standards while maintaining availability on older devices.
