Our Story

Wordly Games is an independent Android development studio based in Clermont, Florida. Founded with a passion for classic brain teasers, the goal is simple: create clean, engaging, and highly polished word puzzles that respect the player's time and intelligence.

The Technology

Every title, including Andygrams Anagram Word Puzzles and Andygrams Daily Challenge, is built natively from the ground up. By utilizing modern Android frameworks like Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, the games deliver smooth animations, responsive interfaces, and a lightweight footprint on your device.
